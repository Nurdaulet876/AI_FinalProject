# AI_FinalProject
**Project Title: SmartDocument AI – Intelligent Document Analysis Assistant**

**Project Summary:**

SmartDocument AI is an AI-powered platform that helps individuals and businesses understand, analyze, and manage complex documents such as contracts, invoices, and official papers. It addresses the problem of time-consuming manual document analysis and difficulty understanding complicated information. Using LLMs, OCR, and RAG, the platform extracts key information, explains complex terms in simple language, identifies inconsistencies and potential risks, and answers questions based on the uploaded documents.
